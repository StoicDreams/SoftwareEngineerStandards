## Who writes this site

I'm Erik Gassler, a software engineer for over 19 years. I taught myself to program in 2007, and my first job was as the first in-house engineer at a small online merchandise business. Since then I've written shared libraries and a company-wide reporting portal at SoftRock and General Services Corp, worked on support analytics at Microsoft, and now lead UI work at Opyn Health. On my own time I run Stoic Dreams, and I'm building games and a game engine in Rust at Wyrdloom Studios.

In 2023 I published *CASE: Continuous Agile Software Engineering*, a book about the practices I've refined over that time.

[My full story and experience](https://www.erikgassler.com/about)

</webui-sideimage>

## Why this site

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

In every role I've had, the biggest difference in what I delivered came from how I worked, not from the team's process or tools. Small changes, tests written as I went, and code pushed to production often are habits I took with me from one job to the next.

Those habits are what this site is about. Each page will be about something one engineer can choose to do, whatever their level, team, or process, with examples from real work.

</webui-page-segment>
